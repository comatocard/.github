# Comatocard

Programmable payment cards for people and AI agents, enforced by smart contracts on NEAR.

A Comatocard vault is a self-custodial NEAR account that holds stablecoins and issues cards. Each card is a key that can only pay, under a policy the contract enforces on every payment: limits, merchant rules, approvals and counterparty checks. Agents spend on their own, inside limits they cannot change, and every attempt is recorded on-chain.

- **Security first:** policies enforced on-chain, fail-closed checks, an independent contract audit with every high issue fixed.
- **Compliance ready:** audit trail by default, named approvers, sanctions listings with their official source, accountant exports.
- **Built on NEAR standards:** NEP-141 tokens, NEP-171 cards, NEP-297 events, global contracts.
